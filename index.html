4. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an email/password account, and we do not intentionally collect your real name, email address, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, or payment card details through the App, except as stated above (for example, an optional leaderboard display name you choose). Do not put personal data in your display name.

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Fraud prevention, security, and integrity of leaderboard writes
  • Responding to support or privacy requests you send us by email
  • Complying with legal obligations

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Internet / network — load ads and optional daily leaderboard
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — optional permission so AdMob may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. You can choose “Ask App Not to Track”; the App still works and ads may still appear as non-personalized where allowed.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by Google Mobile Ads where available; you can limit ad personalization in system Google/Ads settings

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements via Google AdMob, including banner ads and interstitial ads. Google and its partners may collect and process device and advertising information (including advertising IDs where permitted, IP address, device type, OS and app version, and ad interaction data) to serve, measure, and improve ads. On iOS, personalized use of IDFA depends on your App Tracking Transparency choice. On Android, you can limit ad personalization in device settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you obtained a paid or free distribution of the App through Apple or Google, payment (if any) is processed by the platform; we do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

On-device game data is retained until you delete the App or clear app storage. Leaderboard scores and display names are retained as long as needed to operate daily rankings; we may prune older or inactive entries as the service evolves. Advertising and Google service data are subject to Google’s retention practices. Support emails you send us are retained only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate business or legal requirements.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK).

We share data with Google (Firebase and AdMob) to operate the leaderboard and ads; with Apple or Google as platform distributors; and with other players only the leaderboard display name and score fields you submit. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the service.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed in connection with the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where data is transmitted over the internet, we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our services.
