This policy describes how Sudoku Mix handles information when you use the app. We design the game so you can play without creating a traditional account or providing your real name, email, or payment card in-app.

1. Who we are

Sudoku Mix (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is published by Mobile@SG. This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS application Sudoku Mix and related TestFlight / Staging builds where noted.

Contact for privacy questions: mobileatsg84@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • Most game progress, settings, and stats stay on your device.
  • Optional daily leaderboard uses Firebase (anonymous sign-in + cloud scores).
  • The free app shows Google AdMob ads (banner and interstitial).
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not require an email/password login to play Sudoku.

3. Information we process

3.1 Information stored on your device

The App stores data locally (for example using Apple’s on-device storage such as UserDefaults) so the game can work offline and remember your preferences. This may include:

  • Game progress (wins, board unlocks, best times, achievements, stats)
  • Settings (theme, language, difficulty preference, input style, mistake mode, etc.)
  • In-progress puzzle save (so you can resume a game)
  • A display nickname you choose for the leaderboard (optional; defaults to a short generic name)
  • A local random player identifier used before or alongside cloud identity
  • Ad-related timers (for example, limits on how often interstitial ads may show)

This local data stays on your device unless you submit a daily score or ads/SDKs transmit technical data as described below. Deleting the App usually removes local data.

3.2 Daily leaderboard (Firebase)

If Firebase is configured and you use the daily challenge leaderboard, the App may sign you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication (no email or password collected by us) and store scores in Google Cloud Firestore.

Leaderboard-related data may include:

  • Anonymous Firebase user ID
  • Display name (nickname you set, or a default like “Player XXXX”)
  • Daily puzzle performance: time, stars, hints used, mistakes, score
  • Board size, difficulty, and app identifier (e.g. production vs staging) used to keep boards fair
  • Timestamp of score updates

Leaderboard entries are visible to other players who load the same day’s board rankings. Do not put personal data (real name, phone, email, address) in your display name.

3.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including banner and interstitial ads. Google and its partners may collect and process device and advertising information to serve, measure, and improve ads.

Depending on your device settings and consent choices, this may involve:

  • Device identifiers (including IDFA if you allow tracking on iOS)
  • IP address, device type, OS version, app version, and general location derived from IP
  • Ad interaction and performance data
  • SKAdNetwork conversion signals (as configured by Apple / ad networks)

On iOS, if the App requests App Tracking Transparency permission, you can choose Allow or Ask App Not to Track. If you deny tracking, the App continues to work and ads may still appear, typically as non-personalized or limited ads under Google’s and Apple’s rules.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & privacy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3.4 Information we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not ask for your real name, postal address, or phone number as a condition of play.
  • We do not process payments inside Sudoku Mix for core free play (unless later features are added and this policy is updated).
  • We do not provide a social network feed or contacts access.
  • We do not use your device camera, microphone, or precise GPS for the core puzzle features.

6. Sharing of information

We share data only as needed to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and leaderboard database hosting
  • Google AdMob / advertising partners — ad serving and measurement
  • Apple — App Store distribution, standard platform analytics Apple may collect separately under Apple’s policies
  • Other players — only leaderboard display name and score fields you submit

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the service.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data — retained until you delete the App or clear app data via iOS.
  • Leaderboard scores — retained as long as needed to operate daily rankings; we may prune older days or inactive entries as the service evolves.
  • Ad / Google data — subject to Google’s retention practices.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to a consumer puzzle app (including transport security for network calls to Google services and server-side access rules for Firestore where configured). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Sudoku Mix is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of building profiles. Parents and guardians who believe a child has provided personal information through a display name or other field should contact us; we will take reasonable steps to delete such data from systems we control. Advertising to younger users is subject to Google and Apple policies; personalized ads depend on platform consent settings.

10. International transfers

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google and may process data on servers outside your country (including the United States). By using networked features (leaderboard, ads), you understand that such processing may occur under Google’s terms and safeguards.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking (iOS) — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Sudoku Mix
  • Limit Ad Tracking / personalized ads — also via device settings and, where shown, Google’s ad settings
  • Display name — change or clear the nickname in the App (where available)
  • Local data — delete the App to remove on-device storage
  • Leaderboard — avoid submitting scores if you do not want a public nickname/score entry
  • Access / deletion requests — email us; we will help with data under our control (local data on your phone requires action on your device)

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
